Conference Details

ICGB 2026 is planned as a focused academic gathering for biological science, genomics, biotechnology, microbial technology, and allied research areas.

Theme

Genomics and Biology

Sessions will highlight current research directions in life sciences, molecular biology, microbial systems, biotechnology, and computational approaches.

Participants

Students and Faculty

The conference welcomes research scholars, postgraduate students, faculty members, academicians, and industry professionals.

Format

Talks and Presentations

The program includes keynote lectures, oral presentations, poster sessions, expert interaction, and opportunities for research networking.

Includes

Kit and Certificate

Registered participants receive conference materials, participation certification, refreshments, lunch, and access to scheduled academic sessions.
